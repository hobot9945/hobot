FORCE MAJEURE:
1. Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform any of its obligations in whole or in part, when nonperformance is a direct consequence of any circumstances of irresistible (force majeure) nature, which are beyond the reasonable control of the Parties and occurred after conclusion of this Contract.
2. Under this Contract, force majeure shall include: hostilities, strikes, political sanctions, fires, floods, earthquakes and other Acts of Nature.
3. On the occurrence of force majeure, the Parties shall immediately notify each other in writing with documentary evidence of said circumstances within ten working days.
Failure/untimely notice of such circumstances deprives the Party of the right to rely on them as a fact of nonfulfillment/improper fulfillment of its obligations under this Contract. Properly documented evidence of the occurrence of force majeure circumstances can be a certificate issued by an authorized state body.

OTHER TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
1. The present Contract can be cancelled by mutual written agreement of the Parties.
2. All the amendments and addendums to the present Contract are valid only when issued in writing and signed by both Parties.
3. Any one of the Parties may unilaterally terminate the present Contract, by notifying the other Party about its decision 1 (one) month before termination, but the other Party then has the right to claim on compensation.
The Parties hereby agree that upon the receipt of the termination notice, referred above, they shall perform all their obligations under the present Contract by the date of its cancellation indicated in the respective termination notice.
4. A possible annulment or cancellation of a certain part of the Contract herein does not influence on the validity of the whole Contract.
